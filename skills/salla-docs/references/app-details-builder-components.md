## app-details-builder/Get-Started-App-Builder

# Get Started

### Intro

[Salla Partners](https://portal.salla.partners) is introducing a new feature that allows developers to customize their app store listing page for a stronger presence on the [Salla App Store](https://apps.salla.sa).

Your Salla App details is more than just a page, it is the first impression Merchants will have of your application. By customizing the look and feel, you can showcase your brand identity, highlight the value your app delivers, and make it easier for Merchants to understand why they should install it.

A well-designed listing builds trust, communicates professionalism, and can directly improve conversion rates on the [Salla App Store](https://apps.salla.sa).

### Why use it


<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="On-Brand Presentation" icon="material-two-tone-storefront">
    Use your brand colors, typography, and visuals so your Salla App listing feels unmistakably yours, building instant recognition and trust.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Communicate Value Clearly" icon="material-two-tone-sell">
    Spell out outcomes and use cases in plain language, what merchants gain, how it works, and why it matters so benefits are obvious at a glance.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Increase Installs" icon="material-two-tone-trending_up">
    Organize features, screenshots, and proof (ratings, logos) to reduce friction and help merchants evaluate quickly and install with confidence.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Requirements

- [Local](https://salla.dev/blog/salla-partners-account-verification-for-locals/) or [international](https://salla.dev/blog/partners-account-verification-for-internationals/) verified Salla Partners account
- [Published](https://salla.dev/blog/standards-salla-apps-publications/) Salla Public App

### App Details Builder

Use the drag-and-drop builder to compose a compelling, on-brand app details page in three easy steps.

<Steps>
    <Step title="Step 1">

Open "My Apps" in the Partners Portal and select the app whose details page you want to customize.
        
![SCR-20250903-okqm-1.png](https://api.apidog.com/api/v1/projects/451700/resources/360924/image-preview)
    </Step>

    <Step title="Step 2">
    
On the app details page, scroll to "App Details Builder" and click "Customize Page".

![SCR-20250903-okqm-2.png](https://api.apidog.com/api/v1/projects/451700/resources/360925/image-preview)
    </Step>
        <Step title="Step 3">
        
Click "Add Component" and drag the blocks you need. Arrange, edit content, and preview before saving.
